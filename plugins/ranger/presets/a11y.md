# Preset: accessibility

Make the app usable by keyboard, screen reader, and low-vision users. Verify against the running app and the accessibility tree, not just the source.

Each item is `[applies when] -> what good looks like`. If the `[applies when]` condition is not true of this codebase, **skip the item** — do not invent it.

- **Keyboard traversal** — [any interactive UI] -> every control is reachable and operable by keyboard; no traps; logical tab order.
- **Visible focus** — [keyboard-operable UI] -> focus is always visibly indicated; never `outline: none` with no replacement.
- **Semantic structure** — [any HTML UI] -> real landmarks and heading order; lists are lists; buttons are buttons, not clickable divs.
- **Names & labels** — [inputs, icon buttons, images] -> every control has an accessible name; form fields have associated labels; meaningful images have alt text, decorative ones are hidden.
- **Contrast** — [app defines colours] -> text and essential UI meet WCAG AA contrast in every theme shipped.
- **Dialogs & overlays** — [modals, menus, popovers] -> focus moves in on open and is restored on close; focus is trapped while open; Esc closes.
- **Live updates** — [async status, toasts, validation] -> announced to assistive tech via appropriate roles/live regions.
- **Motion & reduced motion** — [app animates] -> honours `prefers-reduced-motion`; nothing flashes at a seizure-risk rate.
- **Zoom & reflow** — [any layout] -> usable at 200% zoom and narrow widths without loss of content or function.
- **Forms & errors** — [any form] -> errors are associated with their fields and conveyed by more than colour alone.

Prefer fixing the highest-impact barrier on the most-used screen first.
